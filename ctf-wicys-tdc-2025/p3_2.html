P3.2 Limited Attempts

★☆☆☆☆ EASY 15 PTS TUTORIAL WiCyS + Target Defense Challenge 2025: BURROWING THE BUSINESS

>_ 100 attempts, 1-10 — no tricks, no guessing in the dark.

Challenge

Most challenges will allow unlimited tries, but some have a limit due to design constraints. In other words, it might be kinda easy to guess and we can't have you getting away with that.

You'll see "x/x Attempts" at the top of these. For this one, you get 100 attempts.

Note Regarding Other Limited-Attempt Challenges

Regarding challenges that have limited attempts (other than this one): if it has limited attempts, you can probably assume that the flag is hiding amongst a small set of things that look nearly correct. If you have multiple options that you can't decide between since they all look correct, you are definitely missing a piece of information that would make choosing the right answer quite certain. Unlocking a hint may direct you towards that vital piece of information. None of the challenges with limited attempts will require any guessing. There may be things that look correct, but they all have only 1 truly correct answer.

Objective

Guess which number I'm thinking of, 1-10! And then please never try to guess an answer like this again!

NO TRICKS HERE. Please don't overthink this!

Flag Format

A number, nothing more. Example:

  • 1
  • 2
  • 3

Solution

Simply pick a number between 1 and 10 (inclusive). I chose 9 and got it right on the first try.
